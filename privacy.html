Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026 · App: BaZi: Destiny Chart

We built BaZi so that your personal data stays on your device. We do not run servers that receive your data, we have no user accounts, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

What stays on your device

Names, birth dates, birth times and birthplaces you enter, and your settings, are stored only on your iPhone (and in a shared container used by the app’s widget). All calculations happen on the device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Services provided by Apple

  • City search. When you look up a birthplace, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps (MapKit) to find the city, its coordinates and time zone. This is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a subscription is active.
  • Notifications. Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Sharing with a partner

If you choose to send an invitation, the app creates a code containing the name and birth details of the selected profile. You decide whom to send it to; we do not receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions about privacy: somefork@gmail.com.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

Все данные, которые вы вводите (имена, даты, время и место рождения), хранятся только на вашем iPhone. У нас нет серверов, аккаунтов, аналитики и рекламы. Поиск города выполняет Apple Maps, оплату — App Store. Коды приглашения вы отправляете сами. Вопросы: somefork@gmail.com.

Política de privacidad (resumen)

Todos los datos que introduces (nombres, fechas, horas y lugares de nacimiento) se guardan solo en tu iPhone. No tenemos servidores, cuentas, analítica ni publicidad. La búsqueda de ciudades la realiza Apple Maps y los pagos, el App Store. Tú decides a quién enviar los códigos de invitación. Preguntas: somefork@gmail.com.
