FAQ

Why does the app need my birthplace?

BaZi uses true solar time: the hour and day pillars depend on the longitude of your birthplace and its historical time zone.

I don't know my birth time

Turn off “I know my birth time”. The chart is built from three pillars and still gives an accurate reading.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

Open Profiles → Restore purchases. To cancel, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap “Manage subscription” in the app.

How do I check compatibility with my partner?

Open the Couple tab and add your partner’s birth details, or send them an invitation code.
